These are my notes from the boutique course. I write them as I go through
each lesson, and publish a note here once it's polished enough to be useful
to someone else.

## How the notes are organised

- Each **module** of the course is a section on the home page.
- Inside a module, lessons are in course order.
- Use **Previous / Next** at the bottom of a note to read straight through.

> If you spot a mistake, let me know. These notes are a learning record, not
> official course material.
